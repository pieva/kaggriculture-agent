## 1. Problem Statement & Strategic Shift

Prior experiments (X1.9, X1.10) demonstrated that expanding physical surface area by purchasing Q2 dilutes workforce focus, spikes seed/product expenditures, and causes severe capital destruction (X1.10 baseline crashed to ~$800). Diagnostic counterfactual audits proved that **Q0+Q1 alone sustain ~$19.4k baseline cash** under X1.10 logic.

Furthermore, the Milk Pipeline Verification identified three critical bugs in X1.10:
1. Gated cow purchases (Day 20/27) caused cows to reach milk readiness after episode end ($0.0 milk revenue).
2. Farm hand dispatch checked `target == state.farmer_position` instead of hand position `(hx, hy)`, preventing hands from executing `HARVEST` or `FEED`.
3. Market `SELL MILK` orders only sold shed inventory, leaving collected milk trapped in worker hands.

**E12-X1.11 represents a fundamental architectural pivot**:
- **Domain Limit**: Strictly capped at **Q0 + Q1** (50 tiles total). Q2 land expansion is removed.
- **Goal**: Transform Q0+Q1 into an **Economic Density Engine** targeting **$80,000+ final money** by repairing the livestock pipeline, eliminating retail wheat purchases, doubling harvest turnover velocity, and optimizing crop mix by phase.

## 3. Core Architectural Pillars & Levers

### Pillar 1: Early Livestock Bootstrap & Economic Gate (4 -> 8 Cows)
1. **Bootstrap Timing (Day 2–6)**:
   - Early livestock Day 2–6 as an architectural principle (first group of 4 cows placed in Q0/Q1 pastures).
   - Warmup (`first_yield_day = 8`): First milk yield occurs on Day 10–14, producing milk for 15+ active days.
2. **Economic Gate for 4 -> 8 Cows Expansion**:
   - The expansion from 4 cows to 8 cows is NOT a hardcoded schedule. It is gated by an explicit **Economic Gate**:
     $$\text{Gate Passed} \iff \begin{cases} 
     \text{Cash Reserve post-purchase} \ge \$400.0 \\
     \text{Internal Wheat feed supply/production} \ge \text{required feed for 8 cows} \\
     \text{Milk pipeline operational} (\text{Milk harvested \& sold} > 0) \\
     \text{Remaining episode horizon} \ge 10 \text{ days (post 8-day warmup)} \\
     \text{Expected Milk Net ROI} > \text{Crop Opportunity Cost on pasture tiles}
     \end{cases}$$
3. **Engine-Level Pipeline Protocol**:
   - `BUY_ANIMAL` (Market) -> `PLACE` on pasture -> `FEED` daily with grown Wheat -> `HARVEST` Milk on pasture tile when `yield_units > 0` -> `DROP` Milk at shed `(4,4)` -> `SELL` Milk on market.
4. **Hand Worker Dispatch Fix**:
   - Replace `target == state.farmer_position` with `(hx, hy) == target` in `_x19_hand_livestock_support_action`. Hands directly execute `HARVEST`, `FEED`, and `DROP` actions.

### Pillar 2: Dynamic Wheat Feed Sizing & Emergency Retail Policy
1. **Dynamic Feed Surface Sizing (No Hardcoded 4 Tiles)**:
   - The number of Wheat tiles reserved for feed is **dynamically calculated** based on:
     $$\text{Required Feed Surface} = f(\text{cows\_count}, \text{feed\_frequency}, \text{remaining\_days}, \text{shed\_wheat\_inventory})$$
   - Minimum wheat surface allocated to reliably support active cows without creating excessive unmonetized feed surplus.
2. **Emergency Retail Wheat Policy**:
   - `BUY_PRODUCT Wheat` is NOT an automatic refill.
   - Retail Wheat purchases are permitted ONLY as an emergency action when:
     $$\text{Expected Livestock Value Preserved} > \text{Retail Wheat Cost} + \text{Opportunity Cost}$$
   - Example: Preserving a cow from escaping (`consecutive_unfed == 1`) when internal wheat inventory is zero.

### Pillar 3: Phased Dynamic Crop Allocation & Realized Net ROI
1. **Realized Net ROI Crop Selector (No Gross Yield Proxies)**:
   - Crops are selected based on **realized net contribution per tile-day**, NOT gross nominal price:
     $$\text{Net Crop EV / Tile-Day} = \frac{\text{Expected Realized Revenue} - \text{Seed Cost} - \text{Labor Burden} - \text{Water Burden} - \text{Market Price Impact}}{\text{Turnaround Days}}$$
   - Accounts for **Time-to-Cash**: Fast 2-day crops (Wheat/Carrot) generate rapid capital turnover; long crops (Melon/Strawberry) lock capital for 10 days.
2. **Phase-by-Phase Allocation**:
   - **Phase 1: Bootstrap (Days 0–6)**: Fast 2-day WHEAT & CARROT cycles for immediate cash generation.
   - **Phase 2: Growth (Days 7–18)**: High Net EV mix (MELON, TOMATO, CARROT) based on dynamic market prices and remaining horizon.
   - **Phase 3: Endgame Sprint (Days 19–27)**: Cap long 10-day crop seeding after Day 18. Shift 100% of tiles to fast 2-day WHEAT/CARROT to guarantee 100% harvest completion before Day 29.
3. **Harvest Action Priority (`HARVEST FIRST`)**:
   - Ready mature crops and milk tiles take **highest priority** over watering newly planted tiles or moving. Capital is liberated immediately upon maturity.

### Pillar 4: Workforce Locality & Shed Logistics
1. **Worker Team Size**:
   - Hire 4 hands on Day 0, 2 hands on Day 1. Total team = **6 workers** (Farmer + 5 Hands). Stop hires after Day 1 (capped at $2,000 spend).
2. **Spatial Radius Assignment**:
   - Actor-position task assignment: tasks assigned based on proximity to actor `(hx, hy)`.
   - Worker 0 (Farmer): Core logistics, market orders, Q0 center crop harvest/dropoff.
   - Worker 1 & 2: Q0 North/East crop cluster.
   - Worker 3 & 4: Q1 West/East crop cluster.
   - Worker 5: Pasture livestock cluster (feeding, harvesting milk, dropping at shed).
3. **Shed Inventory Dropoff Protocol**:
   - Any worker carrying >= 2 items (or any MILK) routes to `(4,4)` to execute `["DROP", item, qty]` before taking new field tasks.
   - Market order `["SELL", product, qty]` executed every step for all shed products except reserved feed.
