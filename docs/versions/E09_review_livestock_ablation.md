## 1. Executive Finding

The single-variable controlled ablation of the livestock subsystem in **E09-01** demonstrates with high statistical confidence that:

> **In the architecture and test conditions of the 40-tile E08 configuration, the livestock subsystem was a structural net value sink. Removing livestock while preserving all 40-tile crop footprint and scheduling parameters doubled mean final money ($22,899.20 vs $11,468.53, +99.67%), increased median money to $27,672.00 (+176.9%), restored a 100% win rate against standard opponents, and outperformed E08 in 26 out of 30 paired episodes (86.7%).**

Furthermore, E09-01 achieves a median final money ($27,672.00) that **surpasses the shipped E06 baseline ($25,847.00)** and beats E06 head-to-head in **70.0% of episodes (21/30)**. However, its overall mean ($22,899.20) remains -$1,832.17 below E06 due to a severe negative tail in 7 specific seed episodes.

## 9. Failure Mode Diagnosis

Based strictly on empirical log evidence and telemetry, the negative tail in E09-01 is driven by two primary failure modes:

### Failure Mode 1: Day 12 Q1 Land Expansion Cash Bottleneck (`HIGH CONFIDENCE`)
- **Evidence:** In Seeds 500, 700, 100, 200, cash float at Day 12 Hour 0 falls slightly short of the required `$1,000 + $300 reserve = $1,300` threshold due to mid-game seed purchases. `BUY_LAND` Q1 is delayed or missed.
- **Impact:** Q1 land is not unlocked in time for the Day 18 Melon planting cutoff (`melon_plant_cutoff_day = 18`). 14 Q1 Melon tiles remain unplanted, forfeiting ~$22,500 in Melon sales revenue.
- **Affected Episodes:** Idx 15 (Seed 500), Idx 27 (Seed 700), Idx 7 (Seed 700), Idx 5 (Seed 500), Idx 25 (Seed 500), Idx 11 (Seed 100), Idx 12 (Seed 200).

### Failure Mode 2: Rigid Seed Buying Liquid Cash Floor (`MEDIUM CONFIDENCE`)
- **Evidence:** Seed purchasing uses a static `$300` cash reserve floor. When cash fluctuates around $300–$350 near Day 12–15, expensive Melon seeds ($80/pack) are blocked while cheap Carrot seeds ($20/pack) are bought instead, filling empty Q0/Q1 tiles with low-yield Carrots ($35/unit) rather than high-yield Melons ($250/unit).
- **Impact:** Carrot revenue rises to $2,460.00 in bottom runs while Melon revenue collapses to $5,714.29.

## 10. 40-Tile Footprint Interpretation

- **What CAN be concluded:** 
  1. The 40-tile footprint is **NOT intrinsically inefficient**. Freed from livestock, 40 tiles produce a median final money of **$27,672.00**, surpassing E06 (9 tiles) and beating E06 in 70% of episodes.
  2. The failure of E08 was caused by the **livestock subsystem**, not by the 40-tile scale.
- **What CANNOT be concluded:**
  1. We cannot claim that 40-tile execution is fully optimized. In E09-01, worker movement share remains at **60.7%** and average plant pending backlog is **16.07 tiles/day**, indicating that worker travel time across Q0+Q1 still leaves ~30–40% of crop capacity under-utilized during peak growth days.

## 11. Candidate Hypotheses & Primary Experimental Direction

The following candidate hypotheses are registered for future experimentation:

### Candidate Hypothesis 1: Prioritized Q1 Land Expansion Capital Buffer (RECOMMENDED PRIMARY)
- **Observed Failure Mode:** Day 12 Q1 land expansion cash bottleneck (Failure Mode 1).
- **Single Variable Change:** Protect the $1,000 capital required for `BUY_LAND` on Day 12 by capping optional seed purchases on Days 10–11 or giving land expansion absolute capital priority.
- **Next Experimental Question:** *"Se E09-01 protegge il capitale necessario all'acquisto di Q1 al Giorno 12, gli episodi della coda negativa scompaiono senza ridurre le prestazioni degli episodi già forti?"*
- **Expected Result:** Eliminates the 7 severe drop episodes, raising E09-01 mean money from $22,899.20 to **>$26,500.00**, surpassing E06 on both mean and median.
- **Confounding Risk:** Low (purely liquidity timing protection).

### Candidate Hypothesis 2: Dynamic Cash-to-Melon Seed Allocation Policy
- **Observed Failure Mode:** Rigid seed buying cash floor substituting low-ROI Carrots for high-ROI Melons (Failure Mode 2).
- **Single Variable Change:** Prioritize Melon seed purchases over Carrot seed purchases whenever liquid cash permits during Days 1–18.

### Candidate Hypothesis 3: Footprint Size Tuning (e.g. 28–32 Tiles)
- **Observed Failure Mode:** High movement share (60.7%) across 50 owned tiles Q0+Q1.
- **Single Variable Change:** Scale target productive crop tiles from 40 down to 28–32 tiles concentrated around the Q0/Q1 boundary.
